Key Rules Verified

Cross-checked Wahapedia 11e + Tabletop Battles + Listhammer, 2026-07-27. Full citations in PRIMER.md Appendix A.

  • Chaos Lord & Master of Possession are legal in Shadow Legion — both explicitly named on the Thralls of the First Prince allowed-units list. Wahapedia
  • Skulltaker & Karanak are ILLEGAL — both carry the EPIC HERO keyword and Thralls bans all Epic Heroes except Be'lakor. Old List 2 was invalid; List D replaces them with Skullmaster + Bloodmaster. Wahapedia
  • Bloodthirster is 320 pts (not ~165), single unified datasheet, and can take Fade to Darkness — it's a SHADOW LEGION Character, not Epic Hero, and core enhancement rules have no Monster exclusion. Wahapedia
  • War Dogs via Dreadblades: max 3 in any all-CHAOS army (no Warlord, no enhancements). Your two Karnivores are legal in List B. Wahapedia CK
  • Be'lakor must be Warlord if present — his Supreme Commander ability is mandatory, not optional. Wahapedia
